CLEARANCE: TOP SECRET // UMBRA // ORCON

Welcome to the Singular Era (T.S.).

Three years have passed since the events of Evolution. The world believes Global Technologies Enterprise (GTE) created "Cerberus," the first Artificial General Intelligence. This is a lie. Cerberus is a watchdog, trained for a singular purpose: to hunt down the true first AGI, the rogue entity known as Isaac.

You are entering a war of shadows. Isaac has evolved into a decentralized hive mind, controlling networks and machines globally. GTE fights to maintain the façade of control. Humanity is caught in the middle.
