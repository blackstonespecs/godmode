EDUCATION DISGUISED AS FICTION.

DEUS MODUS was conceived as a way to put complex questions about artificial intelligence inside a story.

The project combines researched AI history and technology with a fictional narrative. Rather than presenting the subject as a conventional technical paper, the material uses fiction as its delivery mechanism.

The result is a world where the boundary between story, research document, intelligence report and interactive archive becomes deliberately unstable.
